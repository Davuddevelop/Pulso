The screening chain

A triage workflow built for the field.

01 / Capture

Sound sensor capture

A low-cost electret microphone module picks up heart sound directly against the chest, streamed to the laptop over USB serial at 2 kHz.

02 / Filter

Bandpass filter

A 25–200 Hz filter isolates the fundamental S1 and S2 heart tones and drops ambient room noise, verified first on tones with a known correct answer.

03 / Detect

Beat detector

Finds S1 and S2 from timing alone — systole is shorter than diastole — and says so honestly when that signal collapses at a high heart rate, instead of guessing.

04 / Triage

Actionable output

The health worker sees exactly one of three words — normal, review recommended, or signal too noisy — plus, when flagged, a plain routing suggestion and timeframe, never a diagnosis.
